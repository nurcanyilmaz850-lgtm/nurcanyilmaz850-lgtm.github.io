About Me

I am a career coach, entrepreneurship mentor, and data scientist with a professional background in employment services, career guidance, training, project development, and mentoring.

I continue to expand my skills in data science, machine learning, scientific programming, LaTeX/Overleaf, and introductory astronomy through structured training and hands-on projects.
